Phishing Awareness 🎣

Learn how to recognize and protect yourself from phishing attacks.

🎣 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organization in order to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

Attackers commonly use emails, text messages, social media messages, and fake websites.

⚙️ How Does Phishing Work?

  1. The attacker creates a fake message or website.
  2. The victim receives the message.
  3. The message creates urgency or fear.
  4. The victim clicks a link or provides information.
  5. The attacker obtains the information.

🚨 Warning Signs

  • Unexpected messages asking for personal information.
  • Urgent threats such as "Your account will be closed."
  • Suspicious links or unfamiliar domains.
  • Unexpected attachments.
  • Requests for passwords, PINs, or verification codes.
  • Messages containing unusual spelling or grammar.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

  • Don't click suspicious links.
  • Verify the sender independently.
  • Never share your password or verification codes.
  • Check the website address carefully.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep your software and browser updated.

🚨 If You Receive a Phishing Message

  1. Don't click any links.
  2. Don't open unexpected attachments.
  3. Don't provide personal information.
  4. Report the message if possible.
  5. Delete the message.
