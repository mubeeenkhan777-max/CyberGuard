CYBERGUARD SECURITY GUIDE

Essential Cybersecurity Tips 🛡️

Simple habits that can significantly improve your online security.

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using the same password everywhere.

📱 Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication provides an additional layer of protection even if your password is stolen.

🎣 Watch for Phishing

Be careful with unexpected emails, messages and links asking for passwords, codes or personal information.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Install security updates for your operating system, browser and applications.

🌐 Check Links Before Clicking

Pay attention to the domain name and suspicious URL patterns before opening links.

💾 Keep Backups

Maintain backups of important files so you can recover them if your device is compromised.
